# Example prompts across channels

- With Sprites, how did my ads do last 30 days across every channel?
- Compare Google and Meta CPA last month and say where the next $1,000 should go.
- Use Sprites to find wasted spend in every connected ad account.
- Have Sprites move $50/day from our weakest campaign to our best one, on any channel.
- With Sprites, which campaigns drove the GA4 conversions that dropped last week?
- Ask Sprites what our top 3 competitors are running on Meta and Google.
- Use Sprites to write a blog post for our top Search Console opportunity and draft it in WordPress.
- With Sprites, forecast LinkedIn cost per lead for our ICP before we launch.
- Have Sprites send me a cross-channel summary every Monday at 8am.
- With Sprites, what is connected and how many credits do I have left?

Per-platform prompts are in each platform repository's `examples/prompts.md`.
